Sum up 19 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $250.

This entry is a single-page newspaper front page — "The World in One Day" — drawn for Thursday 19 November 2026. It packs 52 verified stories from the day into one 1024×1024 image: a giant dated masthead, region and field counts, a day-in-UTC timeline, 16 drawn story panels (4 hero + 12 small) each with a region/field tag, a source tag and a two-line deck, a dotted world map with numbered pins, a numbered briefs column for stories 17–52, a "date check" box that names what was left out on purpose, and a sources footer. Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Thursday 19 November 2026; the image says so on the page itself, because the judge has no news feed.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

That writes `out/entry.png` — a 1024×1024 RGB PNG, deterministic (same bytes on every run).
